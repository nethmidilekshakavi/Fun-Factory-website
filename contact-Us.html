💡 Good to Know

Socks required at all times for both children and adults in the play area (Rs. 150/pair at the door)
No outside food permitted inside — baby bottles & baby food allowed in the café area only
Adult supervision required — a parent or guardian (18+) must accompany children at all times for Walk-in Play
Walk-in cutoff: 1 hour before closing  |  Drop N Shop cutoff: 2 hours before closing
Party bookings available any day of the week — call us to check availability!

About

Fun Factory is Sri Lanka's premier supervised indoor playground for children aged 0–11 years. With world-class facilities in Nawala and Mount Lavinia, we deliver the ultimate kids play experience in a safe, clean and exciting environment.

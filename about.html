Who We Are

The Ultimate Kids Venue!

Fun Factory is a unique children's activity centre located in Nawala and Mount Lavinia, designed to cater to both a child's physical and mental development. We are Sri Lanka's first supervised, totally air-conditioned, indoor playground for children aged 0 to 11 years.

With a large jungle gym, dedicated soft play area for toddlers & infants, dramatic play zones, reading corners, and so much more — we offer children the greatest variety in safe and exciting entertainment.

Our fantastic facilities and friendly staff, combined with our reputation for maintaining high levels of cleanliness and hygiene, attracts parents and kids from all over Colombo and beyond.

Rules & Guidelines

Terms & Conditions

Fun Factory has adopted the following policies to ensure a fun and safe environment for everyone. Please read carefully before your visit.

📋

Registration Waiver

A waiver (Registration Form) must be signed by a parent or guardian before children are allowed to play. No exceptions. Fun Factory does not accept liability for any injuries or accidents that occur during play.

🧦

Socks Only Policy

Fun Factory is a socks-only facility. No one — adults included — is allowed past the reception area wearing shoes.

Forgot socks? Purchase a pair at reception for Rs. 150 per pair.
👨‍👩‍👧

Parental Supervision

A parent, guardian, or responsible adult (18+ years) must accompany and supervise children at all times. Staff monitor equipment but are not a replacement for parental supervision.

Adults are welcome in the play area to supervise but are not permitted to use the play equipment.

📏

Age & Height Limits

  • Play facilities are designed for children aged 0–11 years. Children over 11 are not permitted in the play area.
  • The jungle gym has a maximum height limit of 4' 9". Children taller may use all other facilities.
  • No discounts are available for height or age restrictions.
🚫

Zero Tolerance Policy

Fun Factory has a zero tolerance policy for aggressive or unsafe behaviour, including bullying. The facility is monitored by 24/7 video surveillance.

Offending children may be asked to leave immediately without a refund.

🤒

Unwell Children

Please keep unwell children at home, especially those with cold/flu symptoms or unusual rashes. Management reserves the right to refuse entry.

If a child is found to be ill inside the facility, they may be asked to leave — entry charges will be refunded (excludes birthday party guests).

🍕

Food & Drink Rules

  • No outside food or drink is allowed on the premises.
  • Baby bottles and baby food are permitted in the café area.
  • No cakes or party items unless part of a Fun Factory party package.
  • No glass containers ever allowed inside.
  • Children must stay in the designated food area while eating or drinking.
🎱

Ball Pit Rules

  • Ensure little ones wear a diaper if not potty-trained.
  • Wait a bit after food or drink before entering the ball pit.
  • The ball pit is deep-cleaned every week as part of our hygiene routine.
⚠️ A fee of Rs. 5,000/- will be charged for toilet or vomit accidents in the ball pit, due to the labour-intensive clean-up of 15,000+ balls.
ℹ️

General Policies

✅ Fun Factory reserves the right to restrict Walk-Ins when the facility is at maximum capacity or exclusively booked.
✅ Certain play areas may close for emergency maintenance without prior notice or refund.
✅ Customers causing damage to property may be charged for repairs.
✅ Fun Factory does not accept liability for loss or damage to vehicles or property parked on premises.
✅ All opening hours and prices are subject to change without notice.
✅ Fun Factory reserves the right to ask anyone to leave if any rules are broken.
